Academic Reading Passage: Urban Heat Islands

Target Time: 15 Mins

Paragraph A: Metropolitan areas across the globe are experiencing noticeably higher temperatures than their surrounding rural regions, a phenomenon known as the Urban Heat Island (UHI) effect. Concrete, asphalt, and dense building structures absorb solar radiation throughout the day and release it slowly at night, preventing cities from cooling down effectively.

Paragraph B: While industrialization was initially blamed as the sole driver of urban warming, recent climatological studies indicate that the lack of urban vegetation (evapotranspiration) and dark rooftop surfaces account for nearly 65% of the temperature discrepancy. Cities that replaced conventional black asphalt with reflective cool pavements observed an average summer temperature reduction of 1.8 degrees Celsius.

Paragraph C: Furthermore, high temperatures directly escalate energy consumption for air conditioning, which in turn emits additional waste heat and greenhouse gases—creating a self-reinforcing feedback loop. However, municipal authorities in Singapore and Tokyo have successfully mitigated this cycle by mandating vertical rooftop gardens on all new commercial skyscrapers constructed after 2018.

প্রশ্নগুলোর উত্তর দিন (True / False / Not Given & Fill):

1. Industrial factories are the ONLY reason why cities are warmer than villages.

2. Reflective cool pavements lowered summer temperatures by more than 5 degrees Celsius.

3. Tokyo spent more money on rooftop gardens than Singapore in 2018.
